## 1. Executive Summary & Component Scope

SF-03 implements the candidate workspace and tracking experience in Salesforce DX source format (`salesforce/`), establishing explicit, separate views, reports, layouts, and Lightning navigation for finding eligible jobs, resolving unknown eligibility and missing assessments, shortlisting opportunities, tracking active applications, and scheduling follow-ups.

### 1.1 Strict Domain Separation & Non-Mutation Boundaries
In compliance with repository instructions, blueprint §§4–8, and `docs/stories/SF-03.md`:
- **Three Strictly Separated State Domains**:
  1. **Candidate Workflow Domain** (`Application_Progress__c`, `Candidate_Decision__c`, `Candidate_Notes__c`, `Follow_Up_Date__c`, `Interview_Date__c`): Candidate-editable via Standard Edit; changes tracking workflow only.
  2. **Machine Assessment Domain** (`Assessment_Disposition__c`, `Assessment_Reasons__c`, `Relevance_Score__c`, `Score_Scale__c`, `Last_Evaluated_At__c`): Strictly read-only in candidate UI; represents model/rules evaluation outcomes.
  3. **Posting Availability & Source Domain** (`Availability__c`, `Source_URLs__c`, `Job_Title__c`, `Company__c`): Strictly read-only in candidate UI; reflects observed ingestion facts.
- **Assessment Rejection Distinct from Application Rejection**:
  - `Assessment_Disposition__c = 'Rejected'` indicates the machine evaluation rejected the opportunity.
  - `Application_Progress__c = 'Rejected'` indicates the candidate decided to reject or the employer rejected the candidate application.
  - A machine-rejected vacancy can still be shortlisted or applied by the candidate with manual override notes; a machine-eligible vacancy can be rejected or withdrawn by the candidate. Neither state overwrites or derives from the other.
- **Unknown Eligibility Distinct from Missing Assessment & Unknown Availability**:
  - `Assessment_Disposition__c = 'Review'` routes to a dedicated **Review Jobs — Unknown Eligibility / Assessment** queue with prominent conditional text guidance: *"Review required: eligibility or assessment is unresolved. Read the machine reasons. Unknown is neither a pass nor a rejection."*
  - Missing assessment (`Assessment_Disposition__c` is blank/null) is preserved and displayed with distinct guidance: *"Assessment missing — eligibility not established."*
  - `Availability__c = 'Unknown'` indicates unconfirmed source posting availability and is strictly separated from eligibility.
- **Preservation of Deliberate Blanks and Candidate Edits**:
  - Candidate can clear notes, decisions, follow-up dates, interview dates, and progress. Deliberate blanks are preserved and never defaulted back to `'New'` on subsequent ingestion or refresh runs.
- **Zero Org Pollution & Scoped Packaging**:
  - Authored standard Salesforce metadata with no custom Apex, no record-triggered Flows, and no wildcards.
  - Packaged in scoped allowlist manifests: `salesforce/manifest/sf03.xml` and `salesforce/manifest/package.xml`.
- **Live Deployment & Integration Gate**:
  - In strict compliance with repository instructions and authorization boundaries, no unauthorized live org mutations were performed. Offline contract and simulated SYNC-01 preservation checks pass with 100% success; live deployment and RUN-01 live ingestion checks are recorded as **PENDING**.

## 4. UI Specification and User Workflows

### 4.1 Seven Scoped List Views (`Job__c.listViews`)

All views specify `<filterScope>Everything</filterScope>` so that records owned by the background worker user account (`005...`) are accessible to the candidate.

| List View API Name | Candidate Label | Inclusion Predicate | Visible Columns | Primary Workflow Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `JA_Eligible_Jobs` | Eligible Jobs — Machine Assessment | `Assessment_Disposition__c = 'Eligible'` | `NAME`, `Company__c`, `Application_Progress__c`, `Assessment_Disposition__c`, `Availability__c`, `Relevance_Score__c`, `Score_Scale__c`, `Last_Evaluated_At__c`, `Follow_Up_Date__c` | Discovery queue for machine-passed vacancies. Shows unavailable or candidate-withdrawn/rejected records with state visible. |
| `JA_Review_Jobs` | Review Jobs — Unknown Eligibility / Assessment | `Assessment_Disposition__c = 'Review' OR Assessment_Disposition__c = ''` | `NAME`, `Company__c`, `Application_Progress__c`, `Assessment_Disposition__c`, `Availability__c`, `Last_Evaluated_At__c`, `Follow_Up_Date__c` | Triaging queue for ambiguous/unknown eligibility and missing evaluations. Candidate reviews machine reasons. |
| `JA_Shortlist` | Your Shortlist | `Application_Progress__c = 'Shortlisted'` | `NAME`, `Company__c`, `Application_Progress__c`, `Assessment_Disposition__c`, `Availability__c`, `Candidate_Decision__c`, `Follow_Up_Date__c` | Candidate-selected opportunities prioritized for application. Not filtered by machine disposition or availability. |
| `JA_Applications` | Your Active Applications | `Application_Progress__c IN ('Applied', 'Interviewing', 'Offer')` | `NAME`, `Company__c`, `Application_Progress__c`, `Assessment_Disposition__c`, `Availability__c`, `Interview_Date__c`, `Candidate_Decision__c`, `Follow_Up_Date__c` | Active application tracking. Preserves in-flight applications even if the employer closes or cancels the posting. |
| `JA_Follow_Ups_Due` | Follow-ups — Due and Overdue | `Follow_Up_Date__c != '' AND Follow_Up_Date__c <= TODAY` | `NAME`, `Company__c`, `Application_Progress__c`, `Assessment_Disposition__c`, `Availability__c`, `Candidate_Decision__c`, `Follow_Up_Date__c` | Daily action list for recruiter check-ins, interview prep, and task deadlines scheduled for today or past due. |
| `JA_Follow_Ups_Upcoming` | Follow-ups — Upcoming | `Follow_Up_Date__c != '' AND Follow_Up_Date__c > TODAY` | `NAME`, `Company__c`, `Application_Progress__c`, `Assessment_Disposition__c`, `Availability__c`, `Candidate_Decision__c`, `Follow_Up_Date__c` | Horizon tracking for future check-ins and interview rounds. |
| `JA_Tracked_History` | All Tracked Jobs / Application History | None (all accessible records) | `NAME`, `Company__c`, `Application_Progress__c`, `Assessment_Disposition__c`, `Availability__c`, `Candidate_Decision__c`, `Interview_Date__c`, `Last_Evaluated_At__c`, `Follow_Up_Date__c` | Complete historical record access including terminal states (`Withdrawn`, `Archived`, `Rejected`) and legacy blanks. |

### 4.2 Record Layout Specification (`Job__c-JA Candidate Tracking`)

The layout organizes fields into four dedicated sections enforcing cognitive and operational boundaries:

1. **Your Application (Candidate-Editable)**:
   - Fields: `Application_Progress__c`, `Candidate_Decision__c`, `Candidate_Notes__c`, `Follow_Up_Date__c`, `Interview_Date__c`.
   - Behavior: All five fields configure `<behavior>Edit</behavior>`.
   - Standard Edit is the primary supported path; multiline German text and Unicode notes are supported without truncation up to 32,768 characters.
2. **Machine Assessment (Strictly Read-Only)**:
   - Fields: `Assessment_Disposition__c`, `Relevance_Score__c`, `Score_Scale__c`, `Last_Evaluated_At__c`, `Assessment_Reasons__c`.
   - Behavior: All five fields configure `<behavior>Readonly</behavior>`.
   - Null scores are displayed blank (never rendered as 0). Missing reasons are displayed blank (no invented explanation).
3. **Posting Availability and Source (Strictly Read-Only)**:
   - Fields: `Name` (Job Title, Required), `Company__c`, `Availability__c`, `Job_Title__c`, `Source_URLs__c`.
   - Behavior: `<behavior>Readonly</behavior>` (except standard `Name` which requires layout presence).
   - Preserves multiple newline-delimited URLs.
4. **Technical Reference (Collapsed / Read-Only)**:
   - Fields: `Canonical_Job_Id__c`, `Machine_Sync_Version__c`, `Sync_Operation_Marker__c`, `OwnerId`, `CreatedById`, `LastModifiedById`.
   - Behavior: `<behavior>Readonly</behavior>`. Keeps audit metadata out of routine candidate editing.

### 4.3 Lightning Record Page & Guidance Banners (`JA_Job_Tracking.flexipage-meta.xml`)

1. **Highlights Panel**: Positioned in the header region, bound to compact layout `JA_Tracking`. Exposes Job Title, Company, Application Progress, Assessment Disposition, Availability, and Follow-Up Date in a single glance.
2. **Conditional Review Required Banner**:
   - Component: `flexipage:richText`
   - Text: `Review required: eligibility or assessment is unresolved. Read the machine reasons. Unknown is neither a pass nor a rejection.`
   - Visibility Rule: Rendered dynamically when `{!Record.Assessment_Disposition__c} EQUAL Review`.
3. **Conditional Missing Assessment Banner**:
   - Component: `flexipage:richText`
   - Text: `Assessment missing — eligibility not established.`
   - Visibility Rule: Rendered dynamically when `{!Record.Assessment_Disposition__c} EQUAL ''`.
4. **Decision Guidance Notice**:
   - Component: `flexipage:richText`
   - Text: `Your decision changes your tracking workflow only. Source information and machine assessment stay separate.`
   - Displayed unconditionally above the record detail.
5. **Detail Panel**: Positioned in the main region, hosting the standard page layout.

### 4.4 Four Scoped Reports (`Job_Tracker/`)

All reports use `format = Summary` and `reportType = JA_Jobs`:

| Report Name | Filters | Groupings Down | Columns | Required Analytical Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `JA_Eligible_Review` | `D = Eligible OR D = Review OR D = ''` | 1. `Job__c.Assessment_Disposition__c`<br>2. `Job__c.Availability__c` | `CUST_NAME`, `Job__c.Company__c`, `Job__c.Application_Progress__c`, `Job__c.Relevance_Score__c`, `Job__c.Score_Scale__c`, `Job__c.Last_Evaluated_At__c`, `Job__c.Follow_Up_Date__c` | Dispositions remain strictly separate groups. Blank disposition forms its own distinct group (`None`); eligible and review counts are never pooled. |
| `JA_Shortlist` | `Application_Progress__c = 'Shortlisted'` | 1. `Job__c.Assessment_Disposition__c`<br>2. `Job__c.Availability__c` | `CUST_NAME`, `Job__c.Company__c`, `Job__c.Candidate_Decision__c`, `Job__c.Follow_Up_Date__c`, `Job__c.Last_Evaluated_At__c` | Shortlist may include machine-rejected or unavailable jobs; both statuses are visibly projected. |
| `JA_Applications` | None (all accessible jobs) | 1. `Job__c.Application_Progress__c`<br>2. `Job__c.Assessment_Disposition__c` | `CUST_NAME`, `Job__c.Company__c`, `Job__c.Availability__c`, `Job__c.Interview_Date__c`, `Job__c.Follow_Up_Date__c`, `Job__c.Candidate_Decision__c` | Pipeline overview across all progress stages (`New`, `Review`, `Shortlisted`, `Applied`, `Interviewing`, `Offer`, `Rejected`, `Withdrawn`, `Archived`, and legacy blanks). |
| `JA_Follow_Ups` | `Follow_Up_Date__c != ''` | 1. `Job__c.Follow_Up_Date__c` (Asc) | `CUST_NAME`, `Job__c.Company__c`, `Job__c.Application_Progress__c`, `Job__c.Assessment_Disposition__c`, `Job__c.Availability__c`, `Job__c.Candidate_Decision__c` | Chronological action list of scheduled follow-ups across all pipeline stages. |

## 7. Required Integration Check: `SF03-SYNC01-PRESERVE`

The 7-step integration scenario specified in `docs/stories/SF-03.md` was executed offline against `FakeSalesforceGatewayV2`, `SyncService`, and `SyncDispatcher`:

1. **Step 1 — Create via sync path**: Synthetic Eligible/Active vacancy (Job A) and Review vacancy with explicit unknown salary criterion (Job B) were published. External ID mapping created one remote record per canonical ID; creation default `Application_Progress__c = 'New'` applied once. Rejected vacancy (Job C) was not published.
